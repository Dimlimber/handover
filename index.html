01The problem

Why good businesses don’t sell

  • The books don’t tell the real story

    Personal expenses, one-off costs and tax-driven accounting hide what the business really earns. Buyers and banks only pay for profit they can verify.

  • The business is the owner

    If the customers, the know-how and the decisions all sit with you, a buyer sees a job, not a business.

  • There’s no story

    A buyer needs a reason to want this business and a believable way to grow it. A listing with neither gets passed over.

  • The price has nothing behind it

    A number based on what you need to retire won’t survive the first conversation. A number with evidence will.

06Questions

What owners ask us

How do I get my business ready to sell?

Buyers and their lenders look at five things: financial records they can verify, a business that can run without its owner, a customer base that is not concentrated in one or two accounts, operations and paperwork that transfer cleanly, and a price with evidence behind it. Getting ready means fixing the gaps in each before a buyer sees the business. Our free ten-minute readiness score shows you where you stand on all five.

When should I start preparing?

Ideally six to twelve months before you list, because some fixes, such as moving customer relationships to your team, take time. It is still worth doing if you are about to list, or if your business has been listed for a while without selling.

What does Handover cost?

The readiness score and a 45-minute review call are free. The Sale-Ready Package is a fixed fee: from $5,000 for a business worth under $1 million, from $10,000 for $1 million to $3 million, and from $15,000 for $3 million to $10 million. Half is due when we start and half when we hand back. We never take a percentage of your sale.

What do I get for that?

Three years of financials recast to show true owner earnings, a customer and revenue analysis, the written story of your business for buyers, an organised document room, a fix-it list ranked by value, and an indicative value range for today and with the gaps closed.

How long does it take?

Six to eight weeks from kick-off to hand-back, and about five hours of your time: gathering documents, one ninety-minute conversation, and checking the facts in what we write.

Are you business brokers?

No. We do not list businesses, find or speak to buyers, negotiate, or take success fees. We prepare the business and its documents, and you then sell it through the broker of your choice, or on your own. We also do not give formal valuations, or tax or legal advice.

What are recast financials?

Most small-business books are kept to keep tax down, so they understate what the business really earns. Recasting restates three years of results to show true owner earnings (often called seller’s discretionary earnings, or SDE), by adding back the owner’s above-market pay, personal expenses run through the business and one-off costs. Each adjustment is tied to a document, so a buyer’s accountant and a bank can rely on it.

Will anyone find out I am thinking of selling?

No. We deal only with you. We never contact your staff, customers, suppliers or competitors, and nothing you share leaves us without your say-so.

What size of business do you work with?

Owner-run businesses worth up to about $10 million, in any industry. Most of the owners we work with have run their business for twenty years or more.
