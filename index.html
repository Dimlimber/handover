01The problem

Why good businesses don’t sell

  • The books don’t tell the real story

    Personal expenses, one-off costs and tax-driven accounting hide what the business really earns. Buyers and banks only pay for profit they can verify.

  • The business is the owner

    If the customers, the know-how and the decisions all sit with you, a buyer sees a job, not a business.

  • There’s no story

    A buyer needs a reason to want this business and a believable way to grow it. A listing with neither gets passed over.

  • The price has nothing behind it

    A number based on what you need to retire won’t survive the first conversation. A number with evidence will.
