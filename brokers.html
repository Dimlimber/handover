For business brokers

Send us the sellers you can’t list yet.

You meet owners every week whose business isn’t ready for market: books that won’t survive diligence, an owner who is the business, a price with nothing behind it. We get them ready and send them back to you.

01What we do

The preparation you don’t have time for

  • Three years of financials recast, with every add-back documented
  • Customer concentration and revenue-quality analysis
  • A draft information memo you can make your own
  • An organised data room, ready for a buyer’s checks
  • A realistic conversation about price, before you have to have it

02What we don’t do

We are not brokers

  • We don’t list businesses
  • We don’t find or talk to buyers
  • We don’t negotiate
  • We don’t take success fees or any share of your commission

The owner pays us a fixed fee for the preparation. When the work is done, the client comes back to you.

03How referrals work

One email to start

  1. Introduce us

    Send a short email to us and the owner. That’s all we need.

  2. We assess

    The owner takes the free readiness score and has a free 45-minute review with us. If they aren’t a fit, we tell you both.

  3. We prepare, you stay informed

    Preparation usually takes 6 to 12 weeks. We keep you posted throughout, and hand back a business you can take to market.
