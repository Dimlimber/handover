01 · What we do

The preparation you don’t have time for

  • Three years of financials recast, with every add-back documented
  • Customer concentration and revenue-quality analysis
  • A draft information memo you can make your own
  • An organized data room, ready for a buyer’s checks
  • A realistic conversation about price, before you have to have it

02 · What we don’t do

We are not brokers

  • We don’t list businesses
  • We don’t find or talk to buyers
  • We don’t negotiate
  • We don’t take success fees or any share of your commission

The owner pays us a fixed fee for the preparation. When the work is done, the client comes back to you.
