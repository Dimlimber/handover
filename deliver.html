03You get back

Recast financials

Your books were kept to keep tax down. A buyer needs to see what the business really earns. We rebuild three years of results to show true owner earnings, and tie every adjustment to a document, so a buyer’s accountant can follow it and a bank can lend against it.

What’s inside

  • Three years of profit-and-loss, restated on a consistent basis
  • A schedule of every add-back, each linked to the invoice or statement behind it
  • True owner earnings for each year, and the trend
  • A one-page reconciliation back to your tax returns
  • Notes on anything a buyer’s accountant is likely to question

This is not an audit, and we never call it one. It is your own numbers, presented the way buyers and lenders read them.

04You get back

Customer analysis

Buyers worry about customers leaving with the owner. We turn your sales records into evidence that they won’t.

What’s inside

  • How much revenue depends on your largest customers
  • How much comes back every year without being won again
  • How long customers stay, and what they spend
  • Revenue by service or product line, and by season
  • A plain summary of the risks a buyer will raise, and your answer to each

Customer names are never shown to a buyer without your written say-so.

05You get back

Your story, written for buyers

Buyers look at dozens of businesses. The memo is what makes them pick up the phone about yours. We write it from our conversation with you and from the numbers, in plain language, and you approve every word.

What’s inside

  • A one-page summary a buyer can read in two minutes
  • What the business does, for whom, and why it has lasted
  • How it runs day to day, and who runs it
  • The recast financial summary
  • Three realistic ways a new owner could grow it
  • 8 to 35 pages, depending on the size of the business

Your broker can use it as it is, or make it their own.

06You get back

An organised document room

Deals slow down, and sometimes die, while a seller hunts for paperwork. We collect, sort and index everything a buyer’s checks will ask for, and tell you what is missing while there is still time to find it.

What’s inside

  • One private, indexed folder, laid out the way buyers ask for it
  • Every document named and dated consistently
  • A list of what is missing or out of date
  • A check that your lease, contracts and licences can pass to a new owner

You keep the folder. Nothing is shared with anyone until you decide.

07You get back

A fix-it list

Some things are worth fixing before a buyer sees the business. We tell you which, in order of what they are worth to you, and how long each will take.

What’s inside

  • Every gap we found, ranked by its effect on price and on whether a sale closes
  • What to do about each, in plain steps
  • What can be done this week, this month and this quarter
  • What is not worth fixing, and how to explain it instead

The free readiness score gives you a first version of this list in ten minutes.

08You get back

An indicative value range

Most owners have a number in mind. Few have evidence for it. We show you what businesses like yours have actually sold for, apply that to your recast earnings, and give you a range you can defend.

What’s inside

  • A range for the business as it stands today
  • A range with the fix-it list done
  • The earnings figure and the market multiples behind both, with sources
  • What would move you to the top of the range

This is an indicative range, not a formal valuation. If you need one, we will point you to a certified appraiser.
