About

Who you’ll be working with

Handover is Dimitri and Zach. We’ve spent our careers inside companies, working out what makes them valuable and fixing what doesn’t. We work with a small number of owners at a time, and you deal with us directly.

01The two of us

Dimitri

Dimitri started his first business while he was still at university: a coffee company that grew to three locations and thirteen staff. He sold it before he was 25. He bought his second business before he was 30.

So he has sat on both sides of the table. He knows what it is to hand over something you built, and he knows what a buyer looks for before they will pay for it, because he has been that buyer.

Since then he has built a career in strategy consulting, helping large companies work out what makes them valuable and tell that story clearly to boards, investors and buyers. Handover brings the same work to owner-run businesses.

Zach

Zach grew up in a family-run restaurant business, so small business has always been personal to him. He saw firsthand what it takes to keep one running: looking after customers, managing people, watching margins and solving whatever problem shows up that day.

Since then, he has spent more than a decade in strategy consulting and operating roles, including at BCG and Alvarez & Marsal. He has advised companies on how to grow, improve profitability, simplify their operations and understand what really drives the value of their business.

Handover brings those two parts of his background together: an understanding of what it means to build and run an owner-led business, and the experience to look at that business the way a buyer will.

02How we work

A few owners at a time

We take on a small number of businesses at once, so the people you meet on the first call are the people who do the work.

Our fees are fixed and agreed before we start. You’ll never get a bill you didn’t expect, and we never take a share of your sale.

We work alongside your accountant, your lawyer and your broker. We don’t replace any of them. We make sure that what they receive from you is complete, clear and ready for a buyer.

03What we’re not

Plainly

  • We are not business brokers. We don’t list your business or find buyers.
  • We are not appraisers. Value ranges we give are indicative, not formal valuations.
  • We are not tax or legal advisers. We’ll tell you when you need one.
